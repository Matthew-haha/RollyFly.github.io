Nine desks, nine chairs and the teacher’s area move from straight rows into two circles, one piece at a time and without a single collision. Press the button under the board to begin.

Lesson notes

What happens on the board

  1. Everything moves to the walls

    Every desk and chair clears the floor first, so the middle of the room is empty.

  2. Desks form the inner circle

    One desk at a time takes its place, following a path that avoids everything else.

  3. Chairs form the outer circle

    Each chair then settles around its desk, facing the centre of the room.

Can you spot these?

  • Only one piece of furniture moves at a time.
  • Nothing overlaps and nothing passes through a wall.
  • Both circles share the same centre.
