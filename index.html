About RollyFly

RollyFly is building the Internet of Furniture: everyday furniture that is connected, aware of its surroundings and able to move on its own. We start in the classroom, where rearranging desks and chairs costs teachers, staff and students time every day.

  • Works with what you have

    We install a compact mobility system on existing or new classroom tables and chairs.

  • Made for education

    Designed for primary and secondary schools, universities and colleges.

  • Safety first

    Every unit senses people and objects, and locks its wheels in an emergency.

How RollyFly works

A compact mobility system (motors, wheels, sensors and wireless control) goes onto existing or new classroom tables and chairs.

  1. Install the mobility kit

    Fit it to the desks and chairs you already have, or choose new furniture that comes with it.

  2. Pick a layout

    Choose rows, circles, groups or any saved layout from a laptop or phone.

  3. Tap once

    Every desk and chair moves itself into place in about 60 seconds.

Key features

Everything the room needs to reconfigure itself, and to stay safe while it does.

  • One-tap room rearrangement

    Change a classroom from rows to circles, groups, or any saved layout in about 60 seconds.

  • Auto-docking chairs

    When class ends, chairs automatically tuck themselves under the desks, keeping the room tidy.

  • App & laptop control

    Teachers and managers control every desk and chair from a simple interface. Layouts can be saved and recalled instantly.

  • Safety lock system

    In an emergency, such as a small earthquake or power cut, the wheels automatically lock so furniture cannot roll.

  • Obstacle avoidance

    Each unit senses people and objects and stops or goes around them, so furniture never collides.

  • Inclusive design

    A remote or app button can call an empty chair to a student who has difficulty walking.

  • Wireless charging

    Furniture can charge automatically when parked, with under-wheel or side charging options.

  • Asset tracking

    Every desk and chair has an identity, so schools can see which items are broken, missing, or need maintenance.

Built to be safe around students

Moving furniture only works in a classroom if everyone can trust it.

  • Stops before impact

    Furniture stops before hitting people or objects.

  • Emergency wheel-lock

    The wheels lock automatically during a power loss or an earthquake.

  • No riding

    Designed so students cannot “ride” the chairs when someone is sitting.

  • Proof on request

    Schools can request certified safety test results before adoption.
